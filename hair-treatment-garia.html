Common Questions

Hair Treatment FAQs

How long does keratin treatment last in Kolkata's humidity?

With proper care and sulphate-free shampoo, keratin lasts 3–5 months even in Kolkata's humid climate. We provide aftercare guidance with every treatment.

Are the hair colour products safe and ammonia-free?

Yes — we use professional-grade, ammonia-free colour brands that are gentler on hair while delivering vibrant, long-lasting results.

How is Charmed different from Green Trends or Toni & Guy?

We offer the same professional quality as chain salons but with personalised attention, local Garia pricing, and stylists who take time to understand your hair type and goals.

Can men get hair treatments at Charmed?

Absolutely. We offer men's hair colour, hair spa, head massage and keratin for men too. Charmed is a true unisex salon.

Do you serve clients from Sonarpur and Narendrapur?

Yes — many of our hair treatment clients travel from Sonarpur, Narendrapur, Baghajatin, Patuli, Bansdroni and across South Kolkata to visit us in Garia.
