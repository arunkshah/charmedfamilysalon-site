Sonarpur Men — FAQs

How far is your salon from Sonarpur?

Our Garia studio is 10–15 minutes from Sonarpur station by car or auto, via the Sonarpur–Garia main road. Easy parking on-site.

Why come to Garia for a men's haircut from Sonarpur?

Most local Sonarpur barbers are stuck in classic styles. Charmed has trained barbers in modern fades, undercuts, textured cuts and styling — worth the 10-minute drive for a real men's salon experience.

Can I walk in or do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome any day 10AM–9PM. Pre-booking on WhatsApp guarantees your slot — useful on weekends when the salon is busiest.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes — kids' haircut for boys at ₹200. Patient, gentle styling for all ages. Many Sonarpur dads bring their boys along on weekends.

Do you do haircut on Sundays?

Yes — we're open every day, 10AM–9PM. Sundays are our busiest day so book ahead via WhatsApp if you have a preferred time.
