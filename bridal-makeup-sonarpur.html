Common Questions

Sonarpur Brides — FAQs

How far is your studio from Sonarpur?

Our Garia studio is around 10–15 minutes from Sonarpur station by car or auto, via the Sonarpur–Garia main road. Many of our brides come from Sonarpur, Rajpur, Kamalgazi and Boral — the route is straightforward and traffic-friendly.

Do you offer pre-bridal sessions for Sonarpur brides?

Yes — our Premium Bride package includes 2 pre-bridal skin glow sessions. Most Sonarpur brides schedule these 4–6 weeks before the wedding for visibly brighter, smoother skin on the day.

Can I book a trial before the wedding?

Absolutely. We strongly recommend a trial session, especially for the Premium Bride package. WhatsApp us to schedule one — usually 2–3 weeks before the wedding.

What's the best time to book during wedding season?

4–6 weeks before your wedding date is ideal, especially during peak season (October–February). Sonarpur brides often book 2–3 months ahead during November–December to secure their preferred dates.

Do you offer at-home bridal makeup in Sonarpur?

For most clients we recommend our Garia studio for the best lighting, equipment and product range. At-home services are case-by-case (and usually for the wedding morning only) — please WhatsApp us to discuss.
