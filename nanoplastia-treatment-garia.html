What it is

What is nanoplastia?

Nanoplastia is a hair-smoothing treatment that reduces frizz and adds shine while keeping more of your hair’s natural movement than re-bonding. People usually choose it when their hair feels rough or puffy, especially in Kolkata’s humidity, and they want it smooth and glossy without a flat, poker-straight look.

At Charmed Family Salon, nanoplastia costs ₹5,500. Prices may vary depending on the density of the hair, and your stylist confirms the final price before starting.

Common Questions

Nanoplastia FAQs

How much does nanoplastia cost in Kolkata at Charmed?

Nanoplastia at Charmed Family Salon in Garia is ₹5,500. Like all our chemical hair services, the final price can vary with hair length and density, so WhatsApp us a photo for an exact quote.

Is nanoplastia better than keratin?

Neither is better for everyone. Both reduce frizz and add shine. Clients usually choose nanoplastia (₹5,500) for a smoother, glossier finish and keratin (₹4,500) when they mainly want frizz control. Your stylist checks your hair and previous treatments before recommending one.

Nanoplastia vs smoothening — what is the difference?

Re-bonding / smoothening (₹4,500–5,000) is meant to make hair straight and sleek. Nanoplastia smooths and adds shine while keeping more of your hair’s natural movement.

How long does nanoplastia last?

It depends on your hair type, how often you wash it and your aftercare. Your stylist will give you a realistic estimate for your hair at the consultation, along with aftercare advice.

Can I get nanoplastia on coloured or previously treated hair?

Often yes, but the stylist needs to check first. Tell us about any colour, keratin, re-bonding or other chemical treatment in the last few months when you book.

How long does the appointment take?

Allow a few hours. Time depends on hair length and density — we confirm it when you book.
