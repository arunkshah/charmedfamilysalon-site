Our Story

About Charmed

A neighbourhood salon for the whole family — built on craft, hygiene and a genuine love for what we do.

Who We Are

A salon that treats everyone the same way — with care.

Charmed Family Salon opened its doors in Garia over seven years ago with a simple idea: a salon doesn't have to be either a barbershop for him or a beauty parlour for her. It can be both, done well, under one roof.

Today we're a fully unisex salon serving men, women, and kids — from a quick beard tidy-up before work, to a full bridal trial, to a child's very first haircut. Same team. Same standards. Same warm welcome every time.

— The Charmed Team

What We Stand For

Four non-negotiables.

Whether you're spending ₹100 on a beard trim or ₹8,500 on a bridal package, these don't change.

Hygiene First

Single-use razors. Sterilised tools between every client. Fresh towels, freshly laundered. No exceptions.

Trained Team

Our stylists, barbers and beauticians are certified — and we keep them sharp with regular training on new techniques.

Premium Products

Trusted, professional-grade brands across hair, skin and grooming. Nothing that damages your hair or your wallet.

Truly Unisex

Equal care, equal time, equal pricing transparency for him, her, and the kids. No section is an afterthought.
