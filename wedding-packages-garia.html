Questions before booking

How long are the Wedding Season Packages valid?

The packages are valid till March 2027. T&C apply. WhatsApp the salon to confirm your dates.

Is makeup included in the bride or groom packages?

No. The Bride to Be, Groom to Be and Couple Combo packages cover skin, hair and grooming services. Makeup is booked separately: party makeup from ₹1,500 and bridal makeup from ₹10,000.

Can we book the Couple Combo together?

Yes. Couple Combos pair her package with his package at a lower price than booking both separately, saving ₹299 to ₹499.

How early should we book for a wedding date?

Book as early as you can during wedding season, especially for bridal makeup, so the team can plan your date and timing.

Looking for individual prices? See the full price list or our bridal & party makeup guide.

Ready to book your wedding package?

Send a quick message with your wedding date and the package you like. The salon team will confirm the best slots before you visit.
