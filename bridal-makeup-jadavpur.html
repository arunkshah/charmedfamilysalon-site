Common Questions

Jadavpur Brides — FAQs

How do I get from Jadavpur to your Garia studio?

Take Raja S C Mallick Road or NSC Bose Road from Jadavpur 8B — a straightforward 15–20 minute drive depending on traffic. Most of our Jadavpur brides come by car or pre-booked cab on the wedding morning.

Do you offer trials for Jadavpur brides?

Yes — trials are highly recommended for the Premium Bride package. We schedule them 2–3 weeks before the wedding so you can lock in your preferred look without day-of surprises.

What if I need bridal makeup for an outdoor wedding?

Our HD/airbrush makeup is designed to last through outdoor lighting and warm conditions. Touch-up kits are included in Premium packages for between-event refreshes.

Are you booked out during wedding season?

November–January slots fill 6–8 weeks ahead. WhatsApp us as soon as your wedding date is confirmed — we'll lock your slot with a small advance.

Can I get pre-bridal sessions even if I book Classic Bride?

Yes — pre-bridal facials and detan can be added to the Classic Bride package as add-ons. WhatsApp us for a custom quote.
