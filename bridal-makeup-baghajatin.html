Common Questions

Baghajatin Brides — FAQs

How far is your studio from Baghajatin?

Just 5–7 minutes from Baghajatin station by car or auto. Many of our brides walk in from Baghajatin Colony, Bijoygarh and Naktala — the route via Garia Main Road is short and traffic-friendly.

Can my wedding entourage visit with me on the day?

Absolutely. Baghajatin brides often bring family along on the morning. We have parking space and seating for mom, sister and friends — no charge.

What's included in the Premium Bride package?

Premium Bride (₹10,000) covers HD/airbrush makeup, hair styling, draping, 2 pre-bridal sessions, mani-pedi, full body detan and a touch-up kit you can take home for the reception.

Do you do reception and engagement makeup separately?

Yes — our Engagement / Reception package (₹3,500) is a popular choice for Baghajatin brides booking multiple events across the wedding week. Combine with bridal for a discount.

How early should I book?

4–6 weeks before for off-peak; 8–10 weeks during peak season (October–February). WhatsApp us early — slots fill up fast in November–December.
