Common Questions

Keratin FAQs

How long does keratin treatment last?

Typically 3–5 months depending on hair type, wash frequency and aftercare. Use sulphate-free shampoo to extend life — we'll give you product recommendations free with every booking.

What's the difference between keratin and smoothening?

Keratin smoothens and reduces frizz while keeping natural movement and bounce. Smoothening (rebonding) makes hair pin-straight permanently. Keratin is gentler — better for fine or coloured hair.

How much does keratin treatment cost in Garia?

Starts at ₹3,000 and varies by hair length and brand. We use premium products — no shortcuts on the chemistry. WhatsApp us a photo of your hair for an accurate quote.

How long does the appointment take?

Plan for 3–4 hours at the salon depending on hair length and density. We recommend a weekday morning slot for the most relaxed experience.

What aftercare do you recommend?

Use a sulphate-free, sodium-free shampoo. Avoid tying hair tightly for 72 hours after treatment. Skip heavy oiling for the first week. We provide a full aftercare card.
