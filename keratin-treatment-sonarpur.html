Common Questions

Sonarpur Clients — FAQs

How far is your salon from Sonarpur for keratin?

Our Garia studio is 10–15 minutes from Sonarpur station via the Sonarpur–Garia main road. The treatment takes 3–4 hours, so plan a relaxed weekend morning.

Is keratin worth the trip from Sonarpur?

Most Sonarpur clients say yes — keratin done with cheap products causes long-term hair damage. The difference between premium and cheap keratin shows up 4–6 weeks later, when the cheap version stops working and your hair is left worse off.

How long does keratin last?

3–5 months with proper sulphate-free aftercare. We provide a free aftercare card with every appointment to help you get the longest-lasting results.

Will keratin make my hair pin-straight?

No — keratin reduces frizz and adds shine while keeping natural movement. For pin-straight hair, you want our smoothening (rebonding) service instead.

Can I do keratin during pregnancy?

We recommend avoiding chemical treatments during pregnancy. Many clients book us right after delivery — our hair spa is a safer alternative meanwhile.
