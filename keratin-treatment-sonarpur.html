Sonarpur Hair, Sonarpur Weather

Why your hair frizzes June to September

Damp air is the real enemy of smooth hair in South 24 Parganas. When humidity climbs, dry or porous hair soaks up moisture from the air, the strands swell, and the cuticle lifts. That is the frizz you fight every morning between Rath Yatra and Puja. Keratin fills those gaps and seals the cuticle with a heat-set coating, so the hair stops drinking the air.

The catch is the product. Cheap keratin can leave hair stiff, smelly or brittle within weeks. We use only GK and Cadiveu for keratin, and our lead stylist checks your hair first to decide whether keratin is even the right treatment for you. Sometimes it isn't, and we will tell you so.

Your Appointment

What happens in the chair

Plan for 3–4 hours depending on length and density. Here is how the visit runs from start to finish:

  1. Free consultation. Send a photo on WhatsApp or ask at the salon. We look at your hair's texture, past colour or chemical work, and what you want, then recommend one treatment with a fixed quote.
  2. Patch test. Every chemical service starts with a patch test, so nothing surprises your scalp.
  3. Wash, apply, rest. Your hair is washed clean and the keratin goes on section by section, then rests for the time that brand needs.
  4. Seal. The treatment is blow-dried and sealed with a flat iron at a controlled temperature. This step locks in the smoothness.
  5. Aftercare brief. Before you leave, your stylist explains when to do the first wash and which sulphate-free shampoo to use, so results reach the full 3–5 months.
