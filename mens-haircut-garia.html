Common Questions

Garia Men — FAQs

Do you do modern fades and undercuts?

Yes — our barbers are trained in skin fades, taper fades, undercuts, textured crops and classic styles. Bring a reference photo if you've got a specific look in mind.

How much for a men's haircut in Garia?

Men's haircut starts at ₹200. Beard trim ₹100. We don't charge premium for fades or specific styles.

Do you offer single-use razors for shaves?

Always. Every clean razor shave includes a fresh single-use razor, hot-towel preparation and soothing aftershave. Hygiene is non-negotiable.

Do you do men's hair colour?

Yes — global colour, grey coverage and beard colour available. Ammonia-free options for sensitive scalps. Starts at ₹300 for beard, ₹800 for hair.
