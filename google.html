Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we highly recommend booking an appointment (especially on weekends) to avoid waiting times.

Is there parking available?

Yes, there is convenient street parking available right near the salon on Anandapally Lane.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), and all major Credit and Debit Cards.
