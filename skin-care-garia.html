Your Questions Answered

Skin Care FAQs

Which facial is best for Kolkata's climate?

For Kolkata's humid weather, we recommend a monthly Basic Facial (₹1,000) for deep cleansing and detan treatments to combat sun damage. Our VLCC premium facial is excellent for anti-aging and brightening.

What brands do you use for facials?

We use trusted professional brands including VLCC and O3+ — clinically tested and proven for Indian skin tones. We never use cheap, untested products.

Is the salon hygienic for skin treatments?

Absolutely. We follow strict hygiene protocols — fresh towels for every client, sterilised tools and a clean treatment area. Your skin safety is our priority.

Do men get facial treatments at Charmed?

Yes — we offer Men's Facial & Cleanup (₹350) specifically formulated for men's skin. Many of our male clients come regularly for facials and skin care.

Do you serve clients from Sonarpur, Baghajatin and nearby areas?

Yes — our skin care clients come from Sonarpur, Narendrapur, Baghajatin, Santoshpur, Bansdroni, Regent Park and across South Kolkata for our facial and skin treatments.
